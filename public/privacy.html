Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 • Abu Mafhal Ltd (RC-8979939)

At Abu Mafhal Hub (operated by Abu Mafhal Ltd, registered under Nigerian law RC-8979939), we prioritize the privacy and security of our users. This Privacy Policy details our practices concerning data collection, usage, and protection in compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR).

1. Information We Collect

To provide telecommunications value-added services (VAS), bill vending, and automated virtual account funding, we collect:

  • Identity Data: Full legal name, date of birth, and government identification numbers (NIN/BVN) when requested for KYC compliance.
  • Contact Details: Phone number, email address, and delivery address.
  • Transaction Data: Details of payments made, utility meter numbers, smartcard numbers, airtime/data recipients, and wallet balance records.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device identifiers, and login security timestamps.

2. Purpose and Legal Basis for Processing

Your information is processed to:

  • Process real-time transactions with telecom network operators (MTN, Airtel, Glo, 9mobile) and utility DisCos.
  • Send automated transactional SMS, OTP verifications, and transaction confirmation receipts.
  • Assign and maintain dedicated 9PSB / PalmPay virtual accounts.
  • Fulfill statutory anti-money laundering (AML) and counter-terrorism financing (CTF) regulations.

3. Data Protection and Encryption

All data transmitted to and from Abu Mafhal Hub is encrypted with enterprise-grade 256-bit Secure Socket Layer (SSL/TLS). Passwords, transaction PINs, and sensitive authentication codes are salted and cryptographically hashed.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information. Data is shared strictly with regulated infrastructure partners (e.g. licensed telecom carriers, payment switches, and commercial banking partners) solely for fulfilling your transaction instructions.
